Staff can ask an AI assistant to turn the transcript of an internal meeting into draft notes and a list of actions. The meeting chair edits and circulates the notes; nothing is sent automatically.

At a glance

Purpose
Internal meetings are often left without notes, or with notes in different formats. The tool gives the chair a first draft so that notes are produced soon after the meeting and in a standard format.
